## 🌟 What is OmniSplit?

**OmniSplit** is a new category of modular multi-domain audio processor. While traditional plugins apply effects to the entire audio signal at once, OmniSplit splits your sound in real-time across **6 independent physical and mathematical domains**:

1. **Transient / Sustain**: Separate fast attack spikes from body decay.
2. **3-Band ZDF Filterbank**: 3 independent zero-delay feedback filter bands.
3. **Mid / Side & L/R**: Sum/Difference stereo matrixing and discrete channels.
4. **Dynamic Splitter**: Separate loud peaks from quiet ghost notes and ambient tails.
5. **Spectral & Harmonic**: Separate pitched steady tones from stochastic noise and texture.
6. **Phase & Spatial**: Isolate mono in-phase energy from diffuse out-of-phase ambience.

Each of these **16 real-time audio streams** can then be freely routed into an **8-slot modular effects rack** boasting **35 studio-grade effect algorithms**, an interactive visual cable patching diagram, 4 stereo output buses, and an ultra-deep modulation engine with LFOs, envelope followers, generative Turing machines, loopable XY pads, and an 8-slot live modulation matrix!

- 🥁 **Surgical Drum Processing**: Saturate and crush the transient snap with a vintage Hard Clipper while routing the decaying sustain body into a lush Dattorro Shimmer Reverb.

## 📑 Feature Breakdown & Highlights

### 🎛️ 6 Pre-Processor Splitters
- **Transient Splitter**: Energy-envelope ratio detector with lookahead and complementary reconstruction.
- **3-Band ZDF Filterbank**: LowPass, HighPass, BandPass, Peak, Notch, and Shelf curves with analog drive.
- **Mid/Side & Stereo**: Width and balance controls with isolated L/R feeds.
- **Dynamic Splitter**: Soft-knee dynamic loud vs quiet separation.
- **Spectral Harmonic Splitter**: ZDF tracking filter separating pitched tones from unpitched noise.
- **Phase/Spatial Splitter**: Cross-channel correlation detector isolating mono from diffuse cues.

### 🔌 8 Modular Effect Slots & 35 Algorithms
- **Universal 25-Source Input Matrix**: Select from all 16 Pre-Processor feeds or 8 inter-slot outputs.
- **35 Studio Algorithms**:
  - *Compressors & Gates*: VCA, Opto, FET, Noise Gate.
  - *EQs & Filters*: 4-Band Parametric, Graphic EQ, Tilt EQ, Dual HP/LP, Bandpass/Notch.
  - *Distortions*: Hard Clip, Tube Saturation, Wavefolder, Germanium Fuzz, Octave Fuzz, Overdrive, Tape.
  - *Glitch & Granular*: Bitcrusher, Stutter Repeater, Tape Stop, Reverse Slice, Granular Jitter.
  - *Modulation & Pitch*: Pitch Shifter ($\pm 24\text{ st}$), Frequency Shifter, Stereo Chorus, Flanger, Phaser, Tremolo.
  - *Delays & Reverbs*: Stereo Delay, Ping-Pong Delay, Tape Echo, Room, Hall, Plate, Spring, Shimmer Reverb.
- **14 Musical Tempo Sync Subdivisions**: `Free`, `1/32`, `1/16`, `1/8T`, `1/16D`, `1/8`, `1/4T`, `1/8D`, `1/4`, `1/2T`, `1/4D`, `1/2`, `1 Bar`, `2 Bars`, `4 Bars`.

### 🧭 Modular Audio Routing Diagram
- Interactive drag-and-drop cable patching with visual glowing cords.
- DAG topological sort with automatic feedback loop prevention.
- Mini In/Out rotary gain knobs on every slot.

### 🌊 Modulation Engine & Mod Matrix
- **3 PolyBLEP LFOs**: 0.01 Hz to 50 Hz or beat-synced 1/32 to 8 Bars, with 2000ms exponential smoothing.
- **3 Envelope Followers**: Powered by any of the 16 pre-processor audio sources.
- **2 Turing Machines**: Algorithmic musical shift registers with probability, clock sync, and glide.
- **4 Global Macros & 2 Orbit XY Pads**: Multi-parameter macro knobs and loopable 2D vector pads.
- **8-Slot Live Modulation Matrix**: 22 sources assignable to 75 plugin parameters.

### 🔊 4 Stereo Output Buses
- Route distinct effect chains to **Bus 1 (Main 1+2)**, **Bus 2 (Aux 3+4)**, **Bus 3 (Aux 5+6)**, or **Bus 4 (Aux 7+8)** for complex DAW multi-out stem mixing.
